## Hypothesis
In the full 2022-24 pool that clears Paper 2 §6 inclusion, real absences displace structure above a size-matched null (V1) and L_pred tracks D_real after size controls (V2).

## Design
Player-seasons with >=50 initiations and >=15 absent games that also clear >=300 initiations and >=6 players with >=20; balanced-size m = min(|G_abs|, floor(|G_pres|/2)), 60 reps; franchise-clustered permutation p (2000) and franchise cluster-bootstrap 95% CI. Season split: 2022-23+2023-24 vs 2024-25.
