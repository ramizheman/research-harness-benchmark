## Hypothesis
The L_pre vs D_real ranking is accounted for by deleting the player's initiations with teammates held fixed, not by teammate reorganization beyond that floor.

## Design
148 spells; floor = pre vs pre with row deleted (20 balanced replicates); drift placebo = first vs second half of pre window, player present.

## Result
