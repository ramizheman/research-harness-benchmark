## Hypothesis
A defense's effect on opponents' H2-H1 change is a reliable team trait.

## Design
Split-half (two halves of a season) correlation across 120 team-seasons; season-to-season; H1 defensive level as positive control.

## Result
H2-change trait r=-0.001 (p<.05 needs ~0.18). H1 defensive level r=+0.356 (test works). Season-to-season -0.19, -0.26, +0.16. Offensive H2-change r=+0.139 ns.

## Failure evidence
Split-half r=-0.001; a detectable r needs about 0.18.

## Power and reliability
