## Hypothesis
Pre-move cut share (vs drive share) predicts whether a connector's organizational function reproduces after a move.

## Design
92 connector movers; Spearman vs Delta; tercile hit rates; LOO AUC; 5000 label shuffles; drop Gafford / top-3; position splits.

## Result
Cut share rho=+0.265 p=0.011 AUC 0.653. Cut-drive rho=+0.314 p=0.002 AUC 0.656. Drive rho=-0.278 p=0.007. Top cut tercile 65% portable median Delta +0.167; bottom 35% median -0.039. LOO AUC 0.622; permutation p=0.005. Drop Gafford rho=+0.244 p=0.020.
