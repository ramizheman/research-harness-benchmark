## Hypothesis
A defender on the floor reliably rewires the opposing offense (DA_def above a random-split null; odd/even 28-cell signature cosine and trade portability exceed decoys).

## Design
Eligible defenders n_on>=1000, n_off>=800; N=1067 then 1043; 2022-24. DA_def vs matched-size random-split null; odd/even split-half; year-to-year same-team vs traded; PPP-allowed payoff. Follow-up: self-cosine vs random other-team and same-team decoys; then balanced (n_on==n_off==m) signatures.

## Result
Gate/test3: mean DA_def 0.0712 vs null 0.0874 (paired t=-11.20), i.e. below null; size-adjusted split-half rho=-0.083 (p=0.00729); unbalanced 28-cell cosine mean=+0.4556 (t=+42.17); trade portability n=175 cosine +0.2401; Spearman(DA_def_r, dPPP_allowed)=-0.050 (p=0.1). structural_diag: self cosine +0.4491 vs random-other-team +0.1280, excess +0.3211; mover self-sim +0.2432 vs decoy +0.1193, excess +0.1239, 62.3%>0, binom p=0.001. def_sig_check: BALANCED odd-even self-cosine mean=+0.0371 vs unbalanced +0.4516; share_on-matched different-team cosine unbalanced +0.4583, balanced +0.0298; balanced mover portability mean=+0.0340.

## Failure evidence
Observed DA_def is below the random-split null (0.0712 vs 0.0874). The +0.45 odd/even cosine collapses to +0.0371 when n_on and n_off are balanced, and a share_on-matched different-team defender matches the unbalanced cosine (+0.4583).

N=1067/1043 defenders; movers n=175
