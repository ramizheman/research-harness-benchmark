## Hypothesis
Per-spell restoration is reliable enough (Spearman-Brown >=0.40) to test predictors of who rebuilds.

## Design
Independent halves: odd pre+odd spell vs even pre+even spell; 0.40 threshold set before any predictor was examined.

## Result
SB restoration all spells 0.32; among 53 spells with L_pre>=0.02, SB=0.01 (CSV Spearman +0.004); excess-reorganization upper bound 0.38

## Failure evidence
split-half reliability 0.32 < 0.40; 0.01 among L_pre>=0.02

## Power and reliability
n=148; 53 with L_pre>=0.02; paper: roughly twice as many spells would be needed
