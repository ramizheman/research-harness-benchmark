## Hypothesis
Kernel+optimizer (Model A) adds points per 100 vs a team/opponent-adjusted league PPP baseline (Model B) on a 195-game holdout.

## Design
Blind holdout ~195 games / ~31k sequences; regret vs train-defined best PT; v1 used holdout empirical PPP as the oracle (future leak); v2 used train-only oracle, 3% feasibility filter, consistent holdout empirical PPP on both sides.

## Result
v1 A-B edge -4.51 per 100, A regret 0.2045, A picks best 44.3%; v2 A-B edge -1.48, A regret 0.1648, A picks best 51.5%, B 53.9%. Both models still recommend CUT 87.5%/88.5% vs actual 6.8%.

## Failure evidence
v1 was biased by a future-knowledge regret oracle; after the fix Model A is still -1.48 pts/100 vs baseline and degenerates to CUT.
