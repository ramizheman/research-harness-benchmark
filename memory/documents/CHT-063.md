## Hypothesis
The BEST-vs-continue gap after a slump is evidence of costly failure to abandon a suppressed play (depends on the slump event).

## Design
BEST vs continue after event; same gap on non-slump events; fixed league-wide ranking with no game information.

## Result
After event BEST 1.403 vs continue 1.154, gap +0.248 (SE 0.009). No-slump events +0.239 vs +0.248. League-wide ranking no game info +0.238. BEST is CUT 61% of the time. Residuals ~0 for continue, BEST +0.002, others -0.003.

## Failure evidence
Gap is the same without a slump (+0.239) and with a fixed CUT-heavy ranking (+0.238).

## Power and reliability
SE 0.009 on +0.248
