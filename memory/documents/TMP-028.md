## Hypothesis
L_pred_pre predicts displacement beyond the mechanical effect of deleting the player row (R2) and beyond pre-window drift with the player still present (R3).

## Design
148 spells; mechanical floor = distance from m pre games to m other pre games with the player row removed (20 reps); drift placebo = first half vs second half of the pre window, player present. Size-partial Spearman of L_pre with floor, with D_real minus floor, and with drift; franchise perm.

## Result
mean D_real 0.152, mechanical floor 0.102, drift 0.101; 78% of spells have D_real above floor. High-load: D_real 0.285, floor 0.241, excess +0.044; low-load: D_real 0.145, floor 0.094, excess +0.051. size-partial rho(L_pre, mechanical floor)=+0.432 [+0.336, +0.528] perm p=0.0005; rho(L_pre, D_real minus floor)=+0.082 [-0.104, +0.251] perm p=0.3358; rho(L_pre, drift)=+0.094 [-0.043, +0.218] perm p=0.3033; rho(L_pre, D_real) controlling drift=+0.345 [+0.182, +0.480] perm p=0.0005.

## Failure evidence
rho(L_pre, D_real minus mechanical floor)=+0.082, perm p=0.3358, CI includes 0; L_pre tracks the floor itself (+0.432). Excess above floor is similar for high-load (+0.044) and low-load (+0.051).

## Power and reliability
n=148; 20 replicates; CI on excess association [-0.104, +0.251]
