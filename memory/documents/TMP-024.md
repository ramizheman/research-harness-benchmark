## Hypothesis
When a rotation player sits, Gap = D_A - L_on is a reliable Absorption vs Cascade trait, predicted by bench-profile redundancy, and Absorption preserves PPP.

## Design
N=1385 rotation player-seasons (>=400 on and off possessions), 30 franchises, 2022-24 two-way possessions; odd/even split-half of D_A and Gap; Spearman of bench cosine similarity with -Gap; PPP_off - PPP_on payoff. Balanced-size follow-up N=882 (m = min(n_on, n_off)). Platoon co-substitution diagnostic.

## Result
Gap split-half rho=+0.591 (p=5.43e-127); D_A residualized rho=+0.489; hubs L_on>=0.05 (n=543) Gap rho=+0.599. Mechanism Spearman(agg_sim, -gap_sz)=-0.205 (p=1.13e-14), hubs -0.307. PPP payoff Spearman(-gap_sz, PPP_off-PPP_on)=-0.039 (p=0.144), hubs -0.054 (p=0.209). Balanced: Spearman(Lcos, DA_bal)=+0.345, DA_bal split-half +0.573, gap_bal +0.545. corr(D_A, co_sub_tv)=+0.312 (p=1.26e-32); partial corr(D_A, L_on | co_sub_tv, min_n, u)=+0.335.

## Failure evidence
Bench-redundancy mechanism has the wrong sign (Spearman(agg_sim, -gap_sz)=-0.205); PPP payoff is null (-0.039, p=0.144); D_A correlates +0.312 with platoon co-substitution.
