## Hypothesis
When T substitutes next, Delta T three-point rate tracks Delta O three-point rate at >=0.25 SD, above a clock-matched placebo.

## Design
20,050 treated windows where T substitutes next; types from earlier dates only; 200 placebos pairing Delta T with other-game Delta O in the same period-minute.

## Result
three slope 0.011 SE 0.008 std=0.011 placebo p97.5=0.014 p=0.06; rim 0.024 SE 0.008 std=0.025 placebo p=0.005; tier benchmark 0.051 std=0.052

## Failure evidence
three-point slope 0.011 SD vs 0.25 threshold, inside placebo; rim 0.025 SD is 1/10 of threshold

## Power and reliability
n=20,050; 200 placebos
