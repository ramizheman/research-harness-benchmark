## Hypothesis
In pre-selected absence cases, the leave-one-out predicted change of the absent player matches the realized team change more than sampling placebo and more than teammate-specific predictions.

## Design
8 primary cases (top-3 L_pred and nearest P90/P75/P50/P25/P10) plus 3 supplementary top-3 outside the July core; direction match corr(dpred, dreal) vs sampling placebo and vs each teammate with >=50 pre initiations. Label-shift check: same statistic for every other 2024-25 team over the case dates, plus 2022-23 Davis/Booker references.

## Result
A. Davis LAL 2022-23: L_pred_pre=0.371, D_real=0.271, excess=0.199, above_p95=True, dir_corr=0.938 (cases2 0.881), placebo_p95=0.549, specificity_rank=1. T. Maxey PHI 2024-25: L_pred_pre=0.108, D_real=0.143, excess=0.077, above_p95=True, dir_corr=0.860 (cases2 0.661), departed_major includes J. Embiid. Label-shift: Brunson own +0.67 vs other-29 median +0.11 max +0.81 share>=own 0.03; Wembanyama +0.38 vs median +0.14 share>=own 0.21; Maxey +0.48 vs median +0.15 share>=own 0.28; Davis reference +0.83 share>=own 0.00; Booker +0.73 share>=own 0.00.

## Failure evidence
2024-25 cases (Wembanyama share>=own 0.21, Maxey 0.28) are less distinctive vs other teams on the same dates than clean-label Davis/Booker (0.00); Brunson remains distinctive (0.03).

## Power and reliability
11 cases; 29 other-team placebos per 2024-25 case
