## Hypothesis
Removing holdout leakage from tier training leaves the PO BEST-AVOID gap intact.

## Design
Corrected PO run; split thick vs thin samples; RS holdout comparison. Post-fix cite +0.375 PO / +0.422 RS.

## Result
Gap dropped from .488 to .375. Thick +0.384 vs thin +0.131 (opposite of leakage-explains-the-drop). PO vs RS difference +0.039, Welch p=0.002, 95% CI [+0.014, +0.065], Cohen d=0.12 (n=825 vs 1,732). Continue rates: fully pivoted 0%; 34.6% increased better-play volume; suppressed != best in 1,603/2,779 = 57.7%.

## Failure evidence
Leakage inflated the old gap but a still-large +0.375 remains; thick samples retain +0.384.

## Power and reliability
