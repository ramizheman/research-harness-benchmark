## Hypothesis
Offenses attack more at the bonus threshold and when an opposing big in foul trouble stays on the floor.

## Design
3 vs 4 team fouls (clock>2:30) with 2-3 and 4-5+ placebos; foul-trouble big remaining on court vs guard placebo.

## Result
Bonus FTA +5.08pp (SE 0.19) mechanical; DRIVE +0.31pp (SE 0.21) identical to 2-3 placebo +0.33pp. Foul-trouble big: rim +1.36pp (SE 0.41 t=+3.4); DRIVE +0.47pp t=+1.3; PPP +0.008; occurs on 1.44% of possessions.

## Failure evidence
FTA jump is the rulebook; DRIVE matches the placebo. Foul-trouble exposure 1.44% because coaches bench the player.
