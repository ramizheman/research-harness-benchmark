## Hypothesis
Real on/off absence of a shaper displaces the team fingerprint more than a usage-matched non-shaper, and LOO L(p) tracks observed D_A.

## Design
908 player-seasons with >=400 poss on and off; 96 usage-matched pairs (caliper 0.03); correlation of L with D_A.

## Result
Delta D_A=+0.115, t=6.35, p~7e-9, shaper larger in 81% of pairs; Delta D_pi=+0.011 p~1e-4; r(L,D_A)=+0.60 (shapers +0.79)
