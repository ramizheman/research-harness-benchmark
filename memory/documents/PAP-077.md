## Hypothesis
Adding points scored in the current stint improves held-out prediction of within-period lineup changes beyond clock and stint length.

## Design
Quick GBT profiling, train 2022-23+2023-24, test 2024-25, every possession as unit (not stoppages); labeled as diagnostic not study.

## Result
AUC clock+stint 0.721; adding stint points 0.726; ~1% of actual changes have predicted p>0.5; PPP SD 1.147; median lineup-game offensive poss=4 (p90=15); ~526 poss to detect 10/100 at 2 SE

## Failure evidence
AUC 0.721 vs 0.726; wrong unit (possessions not stoppages) inflates timing AUC; report appendix flags this

## Power and reliability
