## Hypothesis
After a hub sits, teammates restore the deleted structural direction (restoration R) more than a within-pre-window placebo that deletes the player while he is still present.

## Design
148 spells. gate.py: R = <Delta, d>/|d|^2 on unit 28-cell fingerprints, real (late-pre vs spell) vs placebo (early-pre vs late-pre with player deleted); franchise bootstrap. gate2.py (final): cross-fit so d comes from r1 and C2 from r2, removing shared-noise bias found in gate.py.

## Result
gate.py all spells n=148: G2 R real mean +0.580 (median +0.616) vs placebo +0.428, paired difference +0.151 [+0.050, +0.256]; G2 O difference +0.056 [+0.034, +0.080]. L_pre>=0.02 n=53: R difference +0.177 [-0.008, +0.397]; L_pre>=0.05 n=21: +0.198 [-0.072, +0.497]. gate2.py (final) all spells: R real +0.186 [+0.106, +0.271] vs placebo +0.013 [-0.045, +0.072], difference +0.173 [+0.085, +0.262]; T (teammate response distance) difference +0.012 [-0.004, +0.030]. L_pre>=0.02: R difference +0.140 [-0.035, +0.304]; L_pre>=0.05: +0.140 [-0.087, +0.385]. G1 scaling median 0.0070 vs Monte Carlo noise 0.0053, 95th pct 0.0201, sampling null ~0.078.
