## Hypothesis
The raw +9 pp extra substitution rate after opponent changes is an opponent-personnel response of at least 5 pp under full controls.

## Design
299,539 stoppages where T did not change (59,966 treated); LPM of next-stoppage change; CR1 game cluster.

## Result
raw +8.96 pp [+8.53,+9.39]; period x minute +0.69; +stint/fouls +0.18; full +0.83 [+0.40,+1.26]; exclude timeouts +0.78; opponent timeouts only +2.16; 5-poss secondary +0.87

## Failure evidence
clock alone removes 92% of the raw gap; residual +0.83 pp is 1/6 of the 5 pp GO threshold

## Power and reliability
n=299,539; 3,638 games
