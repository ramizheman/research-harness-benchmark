## Hypothesis
Reconstructed 2020-21 and 2021-22 possession data can enlarge the 148-spell pool toward ~270 spells and improve restoration-predictor power without breaking play-type comparability.

## Design
NBA API audit of playbyplayv3 vs gamerotation; 10-game then 15-game-per-season play-type samples vs yy=22; PBP roster proxy for presence; projection from yy=22-24 yield (~49 spells/season). Full reconstruction and gate.py on the expanded pool were specified but not completed.

## Result
Neo4j/raw files: yy=20 and yy=21 have 0 games. API: 1,080 (yy=20) and 1,230 (yy=21) game IDs. gamerotation ~15% success (2/13 yy=20 games; timeouts). PBP roster proxy 11.4 players per team-game (range 9-15) on a 30-game sample. Uncalibrated 15-game play-type sample vs yy=22: PNR 17.2%/15.0% vs 21.9%; SPOT_UP 29.3%/29.1% vs 21.7%. 10-game subType PNR ~0 events both seasons. Projected spells yy=21 ~42-52, yy=20 ~33-45; yy=21+22-24 ~190-200, still below ~270. G3 analog: yy=25 inferrer data dropped split-half from 0.32 to ~0.00.

## Failure evidence
gamerotation unreliable (~15% success); PNR ~0 in subType samples; even both historical seasons project ~223-245 spells, below the ~270 target; full reconstruction and expanded gate.py never finished (build_log last at 800/1230 yy=21 games; no full_edges_hist.parquet; run_expanded_analysis.py has no log).

## Power and reliability
current pool 148; target ~270; yy=21 projected +42-52
