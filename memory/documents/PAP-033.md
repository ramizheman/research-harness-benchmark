## Hypothesis
Additive > irrelevant on destination embedded minutes and on-court net rating survives when travel OLS and medians are fit on a calibration half and scored on holdout.

## Design
Movers with origin features + outcomes randomly split 50/50; 500 seeds; travel OLS on origin_load/stability/origin_usage fit on calibration only.

## Result
500 splits; embedded-rate gap median=+0.116 [10-90% -0.033, +0.248] P(>0)=0.838; net-rtg gap median=+2.926 [+0.541, +5.072] P(>0)=0.960; corr(travel forecast, net rtg) median=+0.178 P(>0)=0.976; corr(delta, net) median=+0.068 P(>0)=0.822

## Failure evidence
