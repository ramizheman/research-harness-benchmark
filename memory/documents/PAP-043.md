## Hypothesis
A first-half matchup ranking identifies same-game counters that outperform continuation.

## Design
Held-out 55 PO series (633 triggers) and 2025-26 RS (2,779 triggers); ranks locked before H2 outcomes; row-mean PPP; opportunity-cost comparison on different possessions.

## Result
Continue 0.853 PO / 0.839 RS; AVOID 0.923 / 1.001; BEST 1.298 / 1.423; BEST-continue +0.445 PO / +0.584 RS; BEST-AVOID +0.375 / +0.422

## Failure evidence
