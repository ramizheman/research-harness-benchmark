## Hypothesis
The travel x fit quadrant (additive vs redundant/missed-need/irrelevant) predicts post-move minutes, usage, and destination on-court net rating.

## Design
198 movers with box-score outcomes from nba_api stats joined to travel_fit_screen.csv; exploratory in-sample quadrants.

## Result
n=198; embedded mpg>=15: additive 0.80, redundant 0.85, missed-need 0.50, irrelevant 0.68; mean dest net: additive -0.63, redundant -0.35, missed-need -2.50, irrelevant -3.58; corr(delta, dest_net)=+0.077; corr(travel_score, dest_net)=+0.155; corr(fit_score, dest_net)=-0.056

## Failure evidence
net-rating gap additive vs irrelevant is large but corr(delta, dest_net)=+0.077; fit_score correlations are negative; in-sample thresholds
