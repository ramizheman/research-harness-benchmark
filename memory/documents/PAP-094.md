## Hypothesis
Blending the model with betting-derived goto (2024-25) and 538 (2016-23) round-specific win probabilities beats pure model on scored (no play-in) Brier.

## Design
Round from seeds; team A's rdN_win as P(win that round); variable alpha by gender/round/confidence/disagreement.

## Result
rd2+goto_conversion method was wrong (goto Brier much worse); rd6 normalization ~0.11 combined 2025 as fallback; variable/new alpha beat pure model (log examples ~0.157 vs ~0.159; new alpha ~0.156 combined)

## Failure evidence
variable/new alpha lower combined Brier than pure model; round-specific goto kept

## Power and reliability
scored games 2016-2025, play-in excluded
