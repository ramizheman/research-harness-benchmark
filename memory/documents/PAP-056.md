## Hypothesis
The teammate with the closest pre-absence initiation profile (or most minutes / initiations / same-position minutes) is the largest gainer in the absent player's primary types.

## Design
Eligible candidates: last-5-games roster with >=100 pre initiations; chance ~1/7.6; 148 spells plus 11 cases.

## Result
profile match 19% (chance 14%); most minutes 3%; most initiations 5%; same position+minutes 7%; 0/6 high-load cases; 2/11 cases (both low-disruption); minutes-gainer during spell matches initiation gainer 66%

## Failure evidence
19% vs 14% chance; 0/6 hubs; only the during-spell minutes rule works (66%) and is not pre-absence

## Power and reliability
n=148; ~7.6 candidates/spell; 6 high-load
