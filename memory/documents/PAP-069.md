## Hypothesis
A point of opponent three-point luck moves second-half possession share less than a point of opponent two-point luck or expected net points.

## Design
89,588 player-games, 4,730 games, 831 players, 2022-26 RS+PO; outcome=100*(H2 share-H1 share); FE team-game x starter and player-season; game-clustered SE.

## Result
opp3 0.134 (SE 0.027); opp2 0.136 (SE 0.034); XNET 0.094 (SE 0.029); opp3-opp2=-0.002 (SE 0.043); opp3-XNET=+0.041 (SE 0.040); own FG luck 0.511 (SE 0.029); raw PM 0.144 (SE 0.013); 1 SD opp3 (4.39 pts) ~0.59 pp ~8 seconds

## Failure evidence
opp3-opp2=-0.002 SE 0.043; weights equal; size is ~8 seconds/SD
