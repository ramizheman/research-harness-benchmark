## Hypothesis
After matching on first-half PPP (caliper 0.10) and requiring control n2>=5, triggered play types still drop more than same-game same-PT controls.

## Design
Same-game opponent control; |PPP1(T)-PPP1(C)|<=0.10; CR1 game-clustered SE and 5,000 cluster sign-flip permutations.

## Result
N=1,136 pairs, G=848; excess +0.408, CR1 95% CI [0.378, 0.438], perm p<0.0002; triggered 1.305->0.751 (drop 0.554); control 1.298->1.153 (drop 0.145); H1 gap +0.007; DEFF~1.0

## Failure evidence
excess +0.408 after H1 balancing; CI excludes 0
