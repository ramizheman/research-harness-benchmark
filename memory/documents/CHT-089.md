## Hypothesis
Post-opener V2 series probabilities and fired levers recover the actual playoff winner on four historical series without leakage.

## Design
MIA-MIL, IND-NYK, ORL-CLE, DAL-OKC. Leakage-safe RS-only kernels (MIA/MIL: 255k 2022-23 RS poss). V1 pregame P(series) vs V2 post-opener.

## Result
MIA 4-1 MIL: V1 0.1% V2 8.7% (MIA won). IND 4-3 NYK: 25.6% to 49.9% (IND won). ORL 3-4 CLE: 45.8% to 65.2% (CLE won; V2 favored ORL). DAL 4-2 OKC: 34.6% to 54.7% (DAL won). V2 always raised the underdog vs V1. Top levers often TOV_AVOID/OREB_PUSH rather than the expected lever; OREB_PUSH +3pp fired from a league prior for both DAL and IND.

## Failure evidence
OREB_PUSH +0.024 PPP with no personnel check (same CUT-everywhere pathology). ORL V2 65.2% favored the loser. Lever column mostly no.
