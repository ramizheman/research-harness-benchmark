## Hypothesis
Higher AVOID share predicts losing, independent of team quality.

## Design
Game-level n=191 then series-level n=50 Spearman of AVOID/process vs outcomes.

## Result
Game-level rho=-0.19 p=0.008 (inflated n, team-quality confound). Series-level rho=-0.26 p=0.07 (n=50, underpowered; ~80 series needed for p<0.05). Low AVOID-share bin 52.6% vs 25-50% bin 37.0%.

## Failure evidence
Series n=50 p=0.07; still confounded by team quality (OKC disciplined and good).

## Power and reliability
n=50 series; stated need ~80 for p<0.05
