## Hypothesis
Defenders who displace the opponent's initiation fingerprint (high D_A) also suppress opponent PPP.

## Design
882 defender-seasons 2022-25; D_A = 1-cos(A_on, A_off) of opponent initiations; dPPP_allowed = PPP_on-PPP_off; ranks only if n_on>=2000, n_off>=1000, mpg>=15.

## Result
