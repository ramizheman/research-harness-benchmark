## Hypothesis
The opponent-luck coefficient is an artifact of game totals or player identity, not who was on the floor when shots fell.

## Design
200 shuffles of makes; next-game first-half luck predicting this game's share change (n=73,010); Q1 luck -> Q1-to-Q2 share.

## Result
league-wide shuffle mean 0.003 (SD 0.020); within game+defense 0.029 (SD 0.017); observed 0.131; none of 200 draws reached 0.131; next-game luck -0.005 (SE 0.020) vs this-game 0.112 (SE 0.022); Q1->Q2 0.193 (SE 0.044)

## Failure evidence
shuffles null; next-game luck -0.005; observed 0.131 outside all 200 draws

## Power and reliability
200 shuffles; n=73,010 next-game
