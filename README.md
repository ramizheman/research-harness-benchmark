# Historical Sports Research Benchmark

This folder turns the basketball research history (March to November 2026) into an asset for testing whether an AI research agent does better with access to past experiments, failures included. It holds:
- an inventory of the history;
- two information-equivalent memories of it, documents and a graph;
- an audit of how far the history's labels can be trusted;
- an analysis of what the history teaches;
- a benchmark design.

The benchmark has not been run.

## What was built

## How to inspect it

1. **Start with** `data_quality_and_classification_audit.md` for what the numbers mean and how far they can be trusted.
2. **Open** `experiment_inventory.csv` in a spreadsheet. Filter on `study_family_representative == experiment_id` to see one row per design. Use `epistemic_status_derived`, `domain_topic` and `study_family_verdict_conflict` to slice it.
3. **For one record:** read `memory\documents\<id>.md`, then follow `source_files_and_provenance` to the original files. For records drawn from chats, use the transcript ids. Paths under the old `Documents\Basketball Strategy\` folder are resolved in `source_paths_resolved_after_folder_move`.
4. **For lists by status and topic:** see `audit\status_lists.md`. For family-level cross-tabulations, see `audit\family_crosstabs.md`.
5. **For the graph:** load the JSONL files into any graph tool, or read them with pandas. Edges with `needs_review: true` (345) have unverified direction or meaning.

From PowerShell, with the audit virtual environment. The commands do not write `__pycache__`:

**Inputs and outputs:**
- **Read-only inputs:** `Research Ledger\raw\*.jsonl`, `Research Ledger\scratch\chats\*.txt`, and the source files the records cite.
- **Writes:** only inside this folder.
- **Manual file:** `audit\study_family_review.csv` is not generated; edit it to change study families.

## What is incomplete

**Labels are not ready to be ground truth.** The audit, section 12, lists what is needed:
- adjudicate the 20 within-family verdict conflicts;
- double-code at least 60 families;
- re-rate in-advance knowability blind;
- have human curators write `what_was_learned`, `ruled_out_directions` and `viable_directions`. All three are NOT RECORDED for every record.

**Fields not recorded:**
- `primary_outcome` is not in the ledger schema;
- independent replication is not recorded for any record;
- sample size, method and dataset tags are missing for 133, 105 and 52 records.

**Possible further duplicates.** The 319 families may still contain duplicates that were reported with different rounding or wording.

**Uncovered source material** (`audit\coverage_check.md`):
- strategy-app `PREGAME_*`, `demo_reports` and the `reports` folder (scoreable predictions);
- the 2026 backtest logs;
- the `_knicks_*` and `_connector_tier_ppp_*` script families;
- exported chats 1 to 5 and 7 to 11;
- most `Documents\March Madness` files;
- two connector reports in `paper2_output`;
- the LAL vs HOU adaptation brief and presentation in Downloads.

**Unreliable dates.** 205 of 384 records are dated by file modification time, which is not reliable enough for chronological memory cutoffs.

**Superseded ledger file.** `Research Ledger\ledger.jsonl` (381 records) dropped 8 records through a supersede bug. It was left unchanged; use `memory\ledger_merged.jsonl`.

## Next experimental step

Run stage 0 of `benchmark_design.md`, starting with label adjudication and double coding (prerequisites 1 and 2). Without those, no replay task has a trustworthy answer key.

Then run the stage-1 pilot:
- 10 replay tasks and 2 prospective challenges;
- one model, conditions A, B and C, one seed.

Its purpose is to check budget limits, citation redaction and contamination probes, and to estimate the task-level variance for the power calculation. The full comparison should not start until the pilot meets its exit conditions.

## Constraints followed

- **Files left unchanged:** no published paper, manuscript, source dataset, existing script or existing record was modified or overwritten. All new work is in this folder.
- **2025-26 exclusion:** the exclusion from the lineup-adaptation analyses carries into the benchmark: replay tasks derived from that project must not use 2025-26 data.
- **No new hypotheses:** no new basketball hypotheses were proposed, and no novelty claims are made. Novelty checks against published sports research are part of the benchmark protocol.
